### Stage 1: the map
- A round map in the top-right corner, about 64 px across by default (config `ui.minimapSize`, 48 to 128), north-up, the player as a small arrow in the middle that turns with their facing.
- Terrain: for each pixel, the top non-air block's map color (`BlockState.getMapColor`), water drawn by depth (darker when deeper), and a light height shade (brighter on the north-west slope, darker on the south-east) so hills read. Default zoom 1 block per pixel (config `ui.minimapZoom`: 1, 2 or 4 blocks per pixel). Unknown or unloaded pixels stay dark navy.
- Frame in the System style (`system-ui-style` skill): a translucent dark-blue disc, a thin cyan ring, a small `N` on the rim.
- Vanilla draws potion effect icons in the top-right: when the player has effects, push the map down below them so nothing overlaps.
- Hide it when the GUI is hidden (F1), on the debug screen (F3), and while any screen is open. Config `ui.minimap` (default `true`) turns it off.
**Checkpoint 1:** build, commit `feat(client): top-right minimap`, push. If you think you have used more than about 60% of your budget, jump to "Finish" now.

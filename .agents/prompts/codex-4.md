## Stage C: attune and travel

- Right-clicking a stone you don't know **attunes** it: stored in player data `projectzero:waypoints` (copied on clone), a System notice "Waypoint attuned: <name>". The name is the item's custom name if it was renamed in an anvil, otherwise the biome name plus a number.
- Right-clicking a known stone opens a **System-style list screen** (`system-ui-style`, same look as the status window) of every stone you know, with dimension, distance, and a TRAVEL button. Sneak + right-click on a stone you placed lets you rename it (simple text box).
- TRAVEL sends a packet; the server checks the stone still exists, then teleports the player on top of it (safe spot check), plays a sound and particles at both ends, with a cooldown (`WaypointRules.COOLDOWN_TICKS`, 30 s default). Cross-dimension travel only between overworld-like dimensions; never into or out of a dungeon or the Library.
- If a stone is broken, it is removed from the saved data, and players who knew it see it greyed out and get it pruned next time they open the list.
- Register packets in `ModNetwork` (bump the protocol version).
**Checkpoint C:** build, commit `feat(waypoints): attune and travel between waypoint stones`, push. Past about 85% of your budget? Go to "Finish".
